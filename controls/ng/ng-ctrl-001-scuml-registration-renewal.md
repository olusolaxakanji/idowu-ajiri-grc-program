## Control Description

The Idowu Ajiri Foundation is classified as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP) under Nigerian AML law. The foundation must:

1. Maintain an active SCUML registration certificate.
2. Submit an annual return to SCUML by 30 June each year, including a summary of the AML/CFT program, transaction statistics, and the number of Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) filed during the year.
3. Designate a Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) and notify SCUML of any change within 30 days.
4. Implement Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) procedures per SCUML guidelines.

## Test Procedure

1. Confirm SCUML certificate is current and not expired.
2. Confirm annual return was filed by the 30 June deadline for the prior year.
3. Confirm current MLRO designation is on file and notified to SCUML.
4. Verify CDD records exist for all active donor and partner relationships.
5. File evidence of certificate and confirmation of annual return in `evidence/[YEAR]/NG-CTRL-001/`.
