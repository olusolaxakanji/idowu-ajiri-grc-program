## Exception Types

- **False Positive** — Name match investigated; confirmed to be a different entity. Document the distinguishing factors (different jurisdiction, DOB, address, etc.).
- **Confirmed Hit** — Match confirmed as the same entity listed on a sanctions list. Funds frozen; legal counsel and board notified; regulatory reporting assessed.
- **EDD Required** — Entity not on a sanctions list but flagged as a PEP or adverse media hit; Enhanced Due Diligence initiated before proceeding.
- **Control Gap** — Screening was not performed in advance of a transaction; retroactive screen completed; root cause identified.

## Regulatory Reporting Obligations

A confirmed OFAC SDN hit requires the foundation to block the transaction and file a report with OFAC within 10 business days (31 CFR § 501.604). Contact legal counsel immediately for any confirmed hit.

A Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) may be required under SCUML guidelines for any transaction linked to a flagged entity in Nigeria. The MLRO must assess within 24 hours.
