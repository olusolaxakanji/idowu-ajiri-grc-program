## 1. Purpose

To ensure the foundation retains documents for the periods required by law, and destroys them systematically thereafter to reduce storage costs and legal risk.

## 2. Retention Schedule

| Document Type | Minimum Retention Period |
|---|---|
| IRS Form 990 and supporting workpapers | Permanent |
| Board minutes and resolutions | Permanent |
| Incorporation documents and bylaws | Permanent |
| Audited financial statements | Permanent |
| Grant agreements | 7 years after grant closes |
| Contracts and agreements | 7 years after expiration |
| Financial records (general ledger, invoices) | 7 years |
| Donor records | 7 years after last gift |
| Employment records | 7 years after separation |
| AML/BSA transaction records | 5 years |
| Screening records | 5 years after last transaction |
| Email correspondence (routine) | 3 years |

## 3. Legal Hold

When litigation, a government investigation, or a regulatory inquiry is reasonably anticipated, the Executive Director issues a legal hold notice. Documents subject to a legal hold are not destroyed, regardless of the retention schedule, until the hold is formally lifted.

## 4. Destruction

Documents at the end of their retention period are destroyed in a manner appropriate to their sensitivity. Documents containing PII are shredded or securely erased.

## 5. Electronic Records

Electronic records are subject to the same retention periods as paper records. Deletion from active systems must be confirmed by the Finance Lead.
