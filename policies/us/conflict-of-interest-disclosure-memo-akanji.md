# CONFLICT OF INTEREST DISCLOSURE MEMORANDUM

**To:** Board of Directors, Idowu Ajiri Foundation
**From:** Olusola B. Akanji
**Date:** May 5, 2026
**Re:** Disclosure of Potential Conflict of Interest — Freelance Writing Services
**Status:** Formal Disclosure — Action Required by Disinterested Board Members

---

## I. Purpose

I am submitting this memorandum to formally disclose a potential conflict of interest in accordance with the Foundation's governance obligations and applicable best practices under IRS guidance for 501(c)(3) organizations.

This disclosure concerns my intent to offer professional freelance writing services through an independent platform, and the possibility that the Foundation may consider engaging those services in the future.

---

## II. My Current Roles at the Foundation

I currently serve the Idowu Ajiri Foundation in the following capacities:

- **GRC Analyst** — responsible for designing and maintaining the Foundation's governance, risk, and compliance program across US and Nigerian jurisdictions
- **Interim Secretary** — responsible for maintaining corporate records, preparing board meeting minutes, and supporting governance documentation
- **Interim CFO** — responsible for oversight of the Foundation's financial operations, including review and authorization of expenditures

These three roles collectively place me in a position of governance, financial oversight, and administrative authority within the Foundation.

---

## III. Nature of the Potential Conflict

I am establishing freelance writing services offered through Fiverr, a professional services platform. My service offerings include:

- Blog posts and newsletters for nonprofit organizations
- Mission-aligned communications, donor content, and program spotlights
- Technical governance and compliance writing

Given that these services are directly relevant to the Foundation's communications needs, it is reasonably foreseeable that the Foundation's leadership may wish to engage my freelance services for writing and communications work.

If the Foundation were to engage and compensate me for writing services, I would be receiving financial benefit from an organization over which I hold significant governance and financial authority. This constitutes a conflict of interest under standard nonprofit governance practice and IRS guidance on private benefit and self-dealing.

---

## IV. My Commitment to Recusal

Consistent with my responsibilities as GRC Analyst and with the Foundation's governance obligations, I commit to the following:

1. **I will not participate** in any board or leadership discussion regarding whether to engage my freelance services.
2. **I will not vote** on any motion related to approving, modifying, or terminating any agreement for my freelance services.
3. **I will not prepare or approve minutes** for any meeting at which my potential engagement is discussed or decided. A disinterested board member should record those minutes.
4. **I will not exercise my CFO authority** to authorize any payment to myself. Any such payment must be reviewed and authorized by a disinterested officer or board member.
5. I will disclose this conflict at the start of any meeting where the matter may arise, and I will physically absent myself from the deliberation if required by the board.

---

## V. Requested Board Action

I respectfully request that the disinterested members of the Board of Directors take the following actions:

1. **Acknowledge receipt** of this disclosure and enter it into the board record.
2. **Determine independently** whether engaging my freelance services serves the Foundation's best interests, and whether the proposed compensation reflects fair market value.
3. **Document the decision** — approval or denial — in the meeting minutes, including the names of the disinterested members who participated in the deliberation and vote.
4. If approved, **establish a written service agreement** specifying scope of work, deliverables, compensation rate, and payment terms before any services are rendered.

---

## VI. Fair Market Value Reference

To assist the board in evaluating whether any proposed compensation is reasonable, I note that my published market rates for nonprofit writing services are:

- Single blog post or newsletter (600–700 words): $60
- Two content pieces (800 words each): $140
- Four content pieces (1,000 words each): $300

These rates are publicly listed on my Fiverr seller profile and reflect current market pricing for professional nonprofit communications writing. They are not preferential rates created for the Foundation.

---

## VII. Documentation

I request that this memorandum be:

- Entered into the Foundation's official records
- Referenced in the minutes of the next board meeting at which it is reviewed
- Retained for a minimum of seven years consistent with nonprofit document retention best practices

---

## VIII. Acknowledgment

By submitting this memorandum, I affirm that:

- This disclosure is complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge
- I understand my obligation to update this disclosure if material facts change
- I am committed to full recusal from any decision-making related to this matter
