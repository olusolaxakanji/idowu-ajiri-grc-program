## 1. Purpose

To prevent the foundation from engaging in transactions with sanctioned persons, entities, or countries, and to establish procedures for responding to a sanctions hit.

## 2. Prohibited Transactions

The foundation may not, directly or indirectly:
- Accept funds from, or transfer funds to, any Specially Designated National (SDN) or entity on the OFAC Consolidated Sanctions List.
- Conduct transactions involving countries or regions subject to comprehensive OFAC sanctions (currently Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Russia/Ukraine specified regions, and others as updated by OFAC).

## 3. Screening Procedure

Screening is performed per `US-CTRL-001`. The Compliance Officer maintains access to the OFAC SDN search portal and reviews OFAC list updates at least monthly.

## 4. Hit Response Procedure

If a screen returns a potential match:

1. **Freeze** — No funds move. Transaction is placed on hold immediately.
2. **Investigate** — Compliance Officer reviews the match within 24 hours to determine whether it is a true hit or a false positive.
3. **False positive** — Document the distinguishing evidence and record in `screening/exceptions/exception-log.md`. Transaction may proceed.
4. **Confirmed hit** — Compliance Officer notifies the Executive Director and legal counsel immediately. Funds are blocked. OFAC is notified within 10 business days per 31 CFR § 501.604. Board is informed at the next meeting or by emergency notice if material.

## 5. Licensing

If the foundation wishes to engage in a transaction that would otherwise be prohibited, it will seek a specific license from OFAC before proceeding. No unlicensed prohibited transaction may occur.
