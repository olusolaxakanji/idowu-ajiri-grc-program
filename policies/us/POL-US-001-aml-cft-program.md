## 1. Purpose

To establish the foundation's written AML/CFT program for US operations, consistent with FinCEN guidance for nonprofits and international charitable organizations.

## 2. Compliance Officer Designation

The foundation designates a Compliance Officer responsible for day-to-day AML/CFT program oversight, staff training, and liaison with US regulatory authorities.

## 3. Know Your Donor (KYD)

Before accepting a donation above USD 10,000 (or multiple related donations that aggregate above this threshold), the foundation collects and verifies:
- Full legal name of the donor
- Address and contact information
- For organizational donors: jurisdiction of formation and CAC/IRS registration number

## 4. Sanctions Screening

All donors, vendors, and grantees are screened against OFAC lists per `US-CTRL-001` and `POL-US-002-ofac-sanctions-compliance.md`.

## 5. Suspicious Activity

Any transaction that appears unusual, lacks an apparent lawful purpose, or involves a party with an adverse sanctions or PEP profile is escalated to the Compliance Officer. The Compliance Officer assesses whether a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed with FinCEN.

## 6. Recordkeeping

Transaction records, screening results, and KYD documentation are retained for a minimum of 5 years per BSA requirements.

## 7. Training

All staff who handle donations or financial transactions complete AML/CFT training at onboarding and annually thereafter.
