## 1. Purpose

To establish the foundation's written AML/CFT program for Nigerian operations in compliance with SCUML requirements applicable to non-profit organizations as Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs).

## 2. Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)

The Compliance Officer is designated as the MLRO. The MLRO's appointment and any change to this designation is notified to SCUML within 30 days.

## 3. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

Before entering into a financial relationship with any donor, partner, or vendor, the foundation performs CDD including:
- Verification of legal name and identity (individual: government-issued ID; organization: CAC registration certificate).
- Assessment of the nature of the relationship and expected transaction volumes.
- PEP screening for all individual donors and key representatives of organizational partners.

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is required for high-risk relationships including PEPs, cross-border transactions above NGN 5,000,000, and any party with adverse media or prior sanctions history.

## 4. Transaction Monitoring

The Finance Lead monitors incoming and outgoing transactions for patterns inconsistent with the foundation's stated purpose. Any transaction that appears unusual is escalated to the MLRO within 24 hours.

## 5. Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR)

Where the MLRO determines that a transaction is suspicious, an STR is filed with SCUML within 24 hours of the determination. Tipping off the subject of an STR is a criminal offence under the MLPPA 2022 and is strictly prohibited.

## 6. SCUML Annual Return

The foundation files its annual return with SCUML by 30 June each year. Support documentation is maintained in `reports/ng/scuml/`.

## 7. Recordkeeping

CDD records and transaction records are retained for a minimum of 5 years from the end of the business relationship, per MLPPA 2022 requirements.

## 8. Training

All Nigerian-based staff complete AML/CFT training at onboarding and annually thereafter.
