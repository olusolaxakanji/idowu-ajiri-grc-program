## 1. Purpose

To ensure the foundation manages Nigerian bank accounts and foreign currency inflows in compliance with CBN requirements for NGOs.

## 2. Bank Account Registration

All foundation bank accounts in Nigeria are held at CBN-licensed commercial banks. Account opening documentation includes the foundation's CAC certificate, SCUML certificate, and board resolution authorizing the account.

## 3. Foreign Currency Inflows

All foreign currency donations received into Nigerian accounts must be:
1. Received through CBN-authorized channels (not informal transfer networks).
2. Reported to the account-holding bank with a description of the purpose of the inflow.
3. Exchanged at the official or NAFEM rate; unauthorized rates are prohibited.

The Finance Lead maintains a log of all foreign currency inflows above USD 10,000 (or equivalent), filed as evidence under `NG-CTRL-003`.

## 4. Transaction Reporting

The foundation cooperates with any CBN or bank request for information about the source or purpose of funds. Unusual account activity is escalated to the MLRO per `POL-NG-001-scuml-aml-cft-program.md`.

## 5. Dual Signatory

All Nigerian bank transactions above NGN 500,000 require authorization by two signatories: the Finance Lead and one of the Executive Director or Board Treasurer.
