## 1. Purpose

To operationalize the foundation's obligations under the NDPR and NDPA for personal data collected or processed in Nigeria.

## 2. Data Inventory

The Compliance Officer maintains a register of all categories of personal data processed by the Nigerian office, the lawful basis for processing each category, and the retention period.

## 3. Consent

Where consent is the lawful basis for processing, it is obtained in clear, plain language before collection. Consent records are retained for the duration of the processing relationship plus 2 years.

## 4. Data Subject Rights

Nigerian data subjects may submit requests for access, correction, objection, or deletion to the Compliance Officer. Requests are acknowledged within 72 hours and substantively addressed within 30 days.

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers

Personal data of Nigerian residents is not transferred outside Nigeria without either the data subject's explicit consent or confirmation that the destination jurisdiction provides adequate protection, per NITDA guidance.

## 6. Breach Notification

A data breach likely to affect the rights of Nigerian data subjects is reported to NITDA within 72 hours of discovery. Affected data subjects are notified without undue delay where required.

## 7. Data Protection Audit

The foundation conducts an internal data protection review annually as part of the annual GRC retrospective.
