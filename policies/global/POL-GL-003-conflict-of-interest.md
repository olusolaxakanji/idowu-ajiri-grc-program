## 1. Purpose

To ensure that foundation decisions are made in the foundation's best interest, free from improper influence by personal or financial interests of individuals involved in those decisions.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all board members, officers, and key employees in both jurisdictions.

## 3. Defining a Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest exists when a covered person has a direct or indirect financial interest in a transaction or relationship that the foundation is considering or has entered into.

## 4. Disclosure Obligation

Covered persons must:
1. Disclose any actual or potential conflict in writing to the board chair or Compliance Officer as soon as it becomes known.
2. Complete an annual conflict of interest disclosure form (template: `templates/conflict-of-interest-disclosure-v1.md`).
3. Recuse themselves from discussion and voting on any matter in which they have a disclosed conflict.

## 5. Board Review

The board, without the conflicted individual present, reviews the disclosure, determines whether a conflict exists, and decides whether to proceed with the transaction. This determination and the vote are recorded in the board minutes.

## 6. Records

Disclosure forms and board determinations are filed as evidence under `GL-CTRL-002`.
