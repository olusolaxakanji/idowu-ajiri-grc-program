## 1. Purpose

The Idowu Ajiri Foundation has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption in any form. This policy defines prohibited conduct and the obligations of all foundation representatives.

## 2. Scope

Applies globally to all directors, officers, employees, volunteers, contractors, and agents acting on behalf of the foundation.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Offering, giving, requesting, or receiving any bribe, kickback, or improper payment — whether cash or in-kind — to or from any person, including government officials.
- Facilitating payments made to expedite routine government actions.
- Using a third party (agent, consultant, partner) to make payments that would be prohibited if made directly by the foundation.
- Misrepresenting the purpose of any payment or expense.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Reasonable, modest, and infrequent gifts or hospitality consistent with local custom are permitted provided they:
- Are not cash or cash equivalents;
- Would not embarrass the foundation if publicly disclosed;
- Are not provided to influence a business or government decision.

Any gift or hospitality above USD 50 (or NGN equivalent) must be pre-approved by the Compliance Officer and logged.

## 5. Due Diligence on Third Parties

Before engaging any agent, consultant, or intermediary, the foundation will conduct due diligence including OFAC screening, reference checks, and a review of any government-official relationships. See `policies/us/POL-US-002-ofac-sanctions-compliance.md` and `policies/ng/POL-NG-001-scuml-aml-cft-program.md`.

## 6. Training

All staff complete anti-bribery training at onboarding and annually thereafter. Training completion is tracked as evidence under `GL-CTRL-003`.

## 7. Reporting

Suspected violations are reported per `POL-GL-004-whistleblower-protection.md`.
