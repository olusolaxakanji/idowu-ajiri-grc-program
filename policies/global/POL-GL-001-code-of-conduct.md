## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards of ethical behavior expected of all directors, officers, employees, volunteers, and contractors of the Idowu Ajiri Foundation, regardless of jurisdiction.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all individuals acting on behalf of the foundation in any capacity, in both the United States and Nigeria.

## 3. Core Standards

### 3.1 Integrity and Honesty
All foundation representatives will act with honesty and integrity in all dealings, internal and external.

### 3.2 Conflict of Interest
Any actual or potential conflict of interest must be disclosed promptly per `POL-GL-003-conflict-of-interest.md`.

### 3.3 Respectful Workplace
The foundation is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation.

### 3.4 Use of Foundation Resources
Foundation assets, funds, and information may only be used for authorized foundation purposes.

### 3.5 Reporting Violations
Suspected violations of this policy should be reported per `POL-GL-004-whistleblower-protection.md`. The foundation prohibits retaliation against good-faith reporters.

## 4. Consequences of Violation

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or engagement, and referral to appropriate authorities.

## 5. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Executive Director and approved by the Board of Directors.
