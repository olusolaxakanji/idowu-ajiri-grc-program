## 1. Purpose

To ensure the foundation collects, uses, stores, and disposes of personal data responsibly and in compliance with applicable privacy laws in Nigeria and the United States.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all personal data processed by or on behalf of the foundation, including donor information, beneficiary records, employee data, and partner contacts.

## 3. Data Minimization

The foundation collects only the personal data necessary for a stated, legitimate purpose. Data is not retained longer than required for that purpose.

## 4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed only where there is a lawful basis: consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, or legitimate interest. The lawful basis is documented for each data category.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Individuals whose data the foundation holds may request access, correction, or deletion of their data. Requests are directed to the Compliance Officer and responded to within 30 days.

## 6. Security

Personal data is stored in access-controlled systems. PII (passports, financial account details, SSNs, BVNs) is never committed to this repository — it is stored in the designated secure drive. See `evidence/README.md`.

## 7. Breach Response

A personal data breach involving Nigerian residents must be reported to NITDA within 72 hours if it is likely to result in risk to individuals' rights and freedoms. Breaches involving US residents are assessed against applicable state breach notification laws. The Compliance Officer leads breach response.

## 8. Third-Party Data Processors

Any vendor or platform that processes personal data on behalf of the foundation must sign a data processing agreement confirming compliance with NDPR and applicable US requirements before receiving data.
