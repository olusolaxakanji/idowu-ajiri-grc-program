## 1. Purpose

To encourage good-faith reporting of suspected violations of law, regulation, or foundation policy, and to protect reporters from retaliation.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all directors, officers, employees, volunteers, and contractors in both jurisdictions.

## 3. What to Report

Any reasonable suspicion of:
- Financial fraud or misappropriation
- Bribery or corruption
- Sanctions violations
- Violations of law or foundation policy
- Endangerment of foundation beneficiaries

## 4. How to Report

Reports may be made:
1. Directly to the Compliance Officer (verbally or in writing).
2. To the Board Chair, if the concern involves the Compliance Officer or Executive Director.
3. Anonymously via the foundation's designated reporting channel (details maintained separately by the Executive Director).

## 5. Non-Retaliation

The foundation strictly prohibits retaliation of any kind against any person who, in good faith, reports a suspected violation or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this policy and grounds for disciplinary action.

## 6. Confidentiality

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible, consistent with the need to investigate and take corrective action.

## 7. False Reports

Knowingly making a false report in bad faith is itself a violation of the Code of Conduct and may result in disciplinary action.
