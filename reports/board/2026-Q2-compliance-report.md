## Executive Summary

Q2 2026 was the Foundation's most active compliance quarter to date. The IRS Form 990 extension was filed May 14, securing a six-month extension to the November 15, 2026 deadline. The SCUML annual return was submitted June 27, three days ahead of the June 30 deadline. The CAC annual return was submitted June 25, five days ahead of the June 30 deadline. All three filings were completed without penalty or deficiency notice. OFAC sanctions screening returned no adverse findings for the second consecutive quarter. The Conflict of Interest Disclosure was completed by all five board members by the April 30 deadline. One MEDIUM risk was closed following SCUML return submission.

The Foundation enters Q3 with no open compliance exceptions, all H1 2026 annual obligations either filed or formally extended, and a clean screening record for the first half of the year.
